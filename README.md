# StudentOS Technologies

> Your academic life, beautifully organized.

StudentOS is a modern student-focused web platform designed to bring learning, productivity, AI assistance, career preparation, and academic resources together in one place.

The project focuses on creating a clean, intuitive, and responsive user experience for students, with a modern dark-themed interface and a complete authentication flow.

---

## 🚀 About StudentOS

StudentOS is designed to support students throughout their academic and career journey.

The platform brings together features and resources that can help students:

- Learn new concepts
- Get AI-powered assistance
- Explore learning resources
- Follow structured learning paths
- Work on projects
- Prepare for careers
- Build resumes and portfolios
- Prepare for interviews
- Explore career opportunities

The goal is to provide students with a single platform where they can learn, build, and prepare for their future.
